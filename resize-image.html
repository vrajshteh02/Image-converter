FAQ

Resizing questions

Type only the width or only the height and the other side is calculated automatically. If you enter both, "Fit inside" keeps the proportions, "Crop to fill" trims the edges to hit the exact size, and "Pad" adds a border instead of cropping.
Most countries use 35 × 45 mm, which is 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI; the US uses 2 × 2 inches, or 600 × 600 pixels. Pick the preset, and the tool crops your photo to the right shape. Always check your country's official rules for head size and background.
Making an image smaller keeps it sharp; the resizer downsamples in several steps to avoid jagged edges. Enlarging beyond the original size cannot add detail and will look softer, so start from the largest original you have.
