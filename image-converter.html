How it works

Three steps. Zero uploads.

  1. 01 / Drop

    Add images or entire folders

    Drag files or nested directories onto the converter. They are read straight into browser memory with the FileReader API.

  2. 02 / Tune

    Pick format, quality & size

Why compress & convert to WebP

Smaller files, faster pages, better rankings.

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that delivers superior lossless and lossy compression. Smaller, richer images make websites load up to 3x faster, directly improving Core Web Vitals and search visibility.

Boost SEO rank & loading speeds

Faster websites perform better in search. WebP compresses files 25–34% better than classic JPEG/PNG equivalents while preserving exceptional clarity.
