FAQ

Compression questions

Choose "Target size", pick a preset such as 100 KB or 50 KB, or type any number, then drop your photo. The tool binary-searches the JPEG or WebP quality to land just under the limit and only reduces the dimensions if even the lowest quality is still too big. This is ideal for job applications, exam forms and government portals.
Compression removes detail the eye barely notices. Around 70–80% quality, most photos look identical to the original while being 60–90% smaller. Very small targets (like 20 KB for a large photo) will visibly soften the image, so use the largest size your form allows.
Yes. By default every image keeps its original format, so a JPG stays JPG and a PNG stays PNG. PNGs are shrunk by reducing the number of colours, which keeps transparency and usually saves 50–80%. You can still pick JPEG or WebP under Output format if you want an even smaller file.
